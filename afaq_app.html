Physiology - Ch.4 - Page 3/12

Cardiac Cycle & Heart Sounds

The cardiac cycle refers to the sequence of mechanical and electrical events that repeats with every heartbeat. It includes systole (contraction) and diastole (relaxation) of the atria and ventricles.


Key Points:

- Systole: Ventricular contraction
- Diastole: Ventricular relaxation
- S1 sound: Closure of AV valves
- S2 sound: Closure of semilunar valves


The duration of one cardiac cycle at normal heart rate (72 bpm) is approximately 0.8 seconds.

During the cardiac cycle, which event occurs immediately after the closure of the semilunar valves?
💡 Explanation

Closure of semilunar valves marks the beginning of isovolumetric relaxation. The ventricles relax without changing volume until the AV valves open.
